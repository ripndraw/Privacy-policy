Privacy Policy for RasenScan

Last updated: April 26, 2026

RasenScan is an unofficial Android app created to help users browse cards, manage collections, build decks, use a score tracker, and scan cards for the Naruto Mythos Trading Card Game.

Information We Collect

RasenScan does not require a user account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, password, payment information, contacts, or location data.

Information Stored on Your Device

RasenScan stores app-related data locally on your device. This may include:

  • Your card collection data
  • Your deck lists
  • Your app settings and preferences
  • Your score tracker history and related app data
  • Your acceptance of app notices, terms, or policies

Permissions

RasenScan may request access to the following device features:

  • Camera: Used only for scanning cards
  • Photos / Media: Used only if you choose to scan a card from an image on your device

How Your Data Is Used

RasenScan uses locally stored data only to provide app features such as collection tracking, deck building, score tracking, match history, and card scanning.

Data Sharing

RasenScan does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. App data is primarily stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share content yourself.

Data Security

RasenScan is designed to store data locally on your device. However, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and users should keep their own backups of important data.

Children’s Privacy

RasenScan is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
